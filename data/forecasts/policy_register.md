### 1b. CGT — Schedule 1 (replaces 50% discount)
Source: KWM, 15 Jun 2026 — https://www.mallesons.com/au/en/insights/latest-thinking/cgt-amendments-a-blast-from-the-past-and-a-new-minimum-tax.html (S); corroborated by https://www.aph.gov.au/Parliamentary_Business/Bills_Legislation/bd/bd2526/26bd067 (P) and https://www.ftconsultants.com.au/negative-gearing-cgt-reform-now-law/ (S).
| Item | Verified detail | Effective | Src |
|---|---|---|---|
| Discount replaced | 50% CGT discount (individuals, trusts, partnerships) replaced by **cost-base indexation + 30% minimum tax** on gains accruing from 1 July 2027 | 1 Jul 2027 | P/S |
| Grandfathering of accrued gains | **Deemed disposal just before 1 Jul 2027 and deemed re-acquisition at market value.** Pre-1-Jul-2027 gain keeps the 50% discount; post-1-Jul-2027 gain uses indexation + min tax | 1 Jul 2027 | S |
| Valuation | Taxpayer can self-determine market value at transition **or** use a Treasurer-determined apportionment method (not yet issued) → mass valuation event | 1 Jul 2027 | S |
| New builds | Investors in new builds may **choose** 50% discount or indexation + min tax | 1 Jul 2027 | P (digest) |
| Pre-1985 assets | Brought into the CGT net for the first time (post-1-Jul-2027 gains) | 1 Jul 2027 | S |
| Not restored | No gain averaging; indexation **not** available to companies | — | S |
| Min tax mechanism | "Minimum tax gap amount" top-up (new s119-10) | 1 Jul 2027 | S |
| No retrospectivity | CGT changes do **not** operate before 1 Jul 2027 | — | S |

**Modelling implication (effect c):** because accrued gains are preserved at 1 July 2027 by the deemed-disposal mechanism, the incentive to *sell before* 1 July 2027 to "lock in" the discount is **weak**. Residual sell-forward incentive only from valuation hassle, post-Budget buyers losing negative gearing in 2027–28, or expectations that the apportionment method will be unfavourable. Treat (c) as a modest listing bulge in H1 2027, not a cliff.

## 2. "Tranche 2" — Treasury Laws Amendment (Tax Reform No. 3) Bill 2026 — **BEFORE PARLIAMENT, NOT LAW**
- **Introduced 14 October 2026** (second reading): https://ministers.treasury.gov.au/ministers/jim-chalmers-2022/speeches/second-reading-speech-treasury-laws-amendment-tax-reform-no-3 (P).
- Exposure draft 4 Aug 2026, consultation closed 21 Aug 2026: https://consult.treasury.gov.au/c2026-792170 (P); explanatory material: https://storage.googleapis.com/files-au-treasury/treasury/p/prj3e46922ceb8760bf4a62f/page/c2026_792170_ng2_explanatory_mat.pdf (P).
- Parliamentary Library digest (~23 Oct 2026): https://www.aph.gov.au/Parliamentary_Business/Bills_Legislation/bd/bd2627/27bd024 (P).
- Being debated in cognate with the *Income Tax Rates Amendment (Minimum Tax on Discretionary Trusts) Bill 2026* and *Tax Reform No. 4 Bill 2026*; Opposition has sought to split out the new-dwelling definition (https://www.timwilsonmp.com.au/treasury_laws_amendment_tax_reform_3_4_income_tax_rates_amendment_bill_2026, S).
- **Status as at 15 Nov 2026: introduced, not passed. CHECK AT START OF EVERY RUN.**

Contents relevant to housing (Schedules 4–5):
| Item | Detail | Src |
|---|---|---|
| "New residential dwelling" (s960-590) | Defined **in the Act** (not by instrument). Qualifies if: (1) built on land with no previous dwelling; (2) non-residential building converted to residential; (3) acquired **within 24 months** of first occupation certificate from a vendor for whom it was new (extended from 12 months in the Budget); (4) a fourth category not captured in available summaries — **verify in Bill text** | P (digest) / S |
| Exemptions | NDIS housing, public housing, build-to-rent; "similar to hotels/hostels" excluded from residential definition | P/S |
| Main-residence first-use | Proposes preserving existing NG/CGT treatment when a main residence is first used to produce income | S |
| Anti-avoidance | Broad, self-executing; applies where tax benefit is *one* purpose | S |
| Apportionment instrument | Draft legislative instrument for real property/non-market-value assets | S |
| Sch 4 | Clarifies CGT reform for complex/trust structures | P |

**Naming note for future agents:** the Treasurer counts the Aug No. 2 Act as "tranche 2"; Treasury's consultation and KWM call the NG/CGT part of the No. 3 Bill "Tranche 2 legislation". The user's brief means the latter. Further tranches are expected (CGT rollovers; foreign/temporary residents; consolidated groups).
