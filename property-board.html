How to read this board. Signals are moving-average trend/momentum pairs computed on the SQM Research weekly asking-price index (combined houses + units) for each city or town — asking prices, not settled sales. Columns: 0–6M (12/4w, 18/6w, 26/9w), 6M–1Y (33/11w, 39/13w, 52/17w), 1–2Y (65/22w, 78/26w, 104/35w), 2–5Y (130/43w, 195/65w, 260/87w), All Signals = every pair. Each pair contributes Trend (price vs slow MA) and Momo (fast vs slow MA); the average maps to Strong Bull > 0.6 > Weak Bull > 0.2 > Sideways > −0.2 > Weak Bear > −0.6 > Strong Bear — the same regime thresholds as the stock board. The stability dot samples the All-Signals regime today and 13 / 26 / 39 weeks back: green = unchanged, yellow = flipped in the last quarter, red = chopping.

Fundamentals: gross rental yield (houses), yield percentile vs the town's own history (higher percentile = cheaper vs itself), rental vacancy rate, 12-month rent growth, and stock on market (total aged listings; rising supply is bearish). Towns are proxied by their canonical postcode; capitals use SQM's city-wide index. Data © SQM Research, personal reference only — this board is decision support, not advice, and asking-price momentum leads settled prices by roughly one to two quarters.

Noise caveat: the 0–6M column reacts within weeks, so it flips often — that's the point of having it, but read it alongside the stability dot rather than on its own.
